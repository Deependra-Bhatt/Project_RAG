# ShopSmart India Category Taxonomy

## Tagging conventions
- Use one primary category and one subcategory for every product.
- Tags are lowercase, comma-separated, searchable terms; include material, use-case, compatibility or key benefit where applicable.
- Do not use competitor names, unsupported superlatives, or promotional claims as tags.

## Electronics
`Electronics > Audio > Wireless Earbuds | Smartwatches | Bluetooth Speakers | Power Banks | USB Chargers`

Required attributes: brand, model, connectivity, battery life, warranty months, seller ID. Earbuds additionally require ANC status, playback time and charging port. Smartwatches require display size, compatibility and water resistance.

## Fashion
`Fashion > Men | Women | Footwear | Accessories > T-Shirts | Kurtas | Jeans | Sneakers | Handbags`

Required attributes: brand, material, size range, colour, fit, care instructions. List standard India size mapping and include a size chart image where sizing applies.

## Home & Kitchen
`Home & Kitchen > Kitchen Appliances | Cookware | Drinkware | Home Furnishing | Storage`

Required attributes: material, dimensions/capacity, included pieces, warranty, power rating where electrical, and care instructions.

## Beauty
`Beauty > Skin Care | Sun Care | Hair Care | Makeup | Grooming`

Required attributes: net quantity, ingredients, skin/hair type, shade where relevant, expiry/shelf-life and safety warning. Do not make medical or therapeutic claims.

## Books
`Books > Fiction | Business & Economics | Self Help | Children's Books | Exam Preparation`

Required attributes: author, publisher, format, language, ISBN, page count and publication year.
