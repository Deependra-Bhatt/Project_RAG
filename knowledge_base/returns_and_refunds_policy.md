# ShopSmart India Returns & Refunds Policy

**Effective date:** 1 September 2026. This policy applies to orders placed on ShopSmart India unless the product page states a more specific statutory warranty process.

## 1. Return windows

Most eligible products may be returned within **7 calendar days** after delivery. Electronics with a manufacturing defect, missing item or transit damage must be reported within **7 days** and are assessed for replacement or refund. Fashion, Home & Kitchen and Books may be returned within 7 days if unused and eligible. Beauty products are returnable only when delivered damaged, expired, incorrect or tampered with; report within 48 hours of delivery with photographs. Personalised, custom-made and made-to-order items are not returnable except for damage, wrong item or defect.

## 2. Eligibility

To be eligible, keep the item unused where applicable, with original packaging, accessories, manuals, labels, freebies and invoice. Products must not be altered, washed, installed or activated unless the claim concerns a verified defect. ShopSmart or its logistics partner may perform pickup inspection. A return can be declined when the serial number does not match, components are missing, or damage is caused after delivery.

## 3. Exceptions by category

- **Electronics:** change-of-mind returns are not available once a sealed personal-use product is opened; eligible defects follow replacement-first assessment. Manufacturer warranty remains available after the return window.
- **Fashion:** sale items remain eligible under the same 7-day window unless clearly labelled final sale. Worn, washed, altered or personalised items are excluded.
- **Home & Kitchen:** installed appliances and hygiene-sensitive items are excluded after installation/opening unless defective or wrong.
- **Beauty:** only damaged, expired, incorrect or tampered products qualify; ordinary skin/hair suitability is not a defect.
- **Books:** incorrect, damaged or print-defect copies qualify; returned books must be unread and undamaged.

## 4. How to request a return

Go to **My Orders > Select order > Return or replace**, select the reason, and upload clear evidence when requested. Requests are reviewed within 2 business days. Do not hand an item to an unverified pickup person; the pickup confirmation in the app is required.

## 5. Refunds

After the return passes pickup/quality review, refunds are initiated within **2 business days**. The usual bank/issuer posting time is 5–7 business days for cards and net banking, 2–4 business days for UPI/wallets, and 7–10 business days for EMI reversals. Cash-on-delivery refunds require a verified bank account or UPI ID. Shipping or convenience fees are refunded only when ShopSmart confirms a wrong, damaged, defective or undeliverable item.

## 6. Warranty and seller support

The return window is separate from manufacturer warranty. After it ends, use the brand warranty process with the invoice and serial number. Third-party sellers must cooperate with valid claims, but ShopSmart does not promise a replacement for damage caused by misuse. Escalate unresolved verified-seller claims through Help Centre within 30 days of the seller's final response.
