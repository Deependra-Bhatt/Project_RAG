# ShopSmart India Seller Onboarding Guide

## Step 1: Create and verify your account
Register with your mobile number and email, then submit PAN, GSTIN where applicable, bank account details, pickup address and authorised-signatory ID. Verification normally completes in 2 business days. Never list before the account status reads **Verified**.

## Step 2: Complete your seller profile
Set your legal business name, support contact, return address and tax settings. Keep bank and tax records current. Seller IDs are assigned after verification.

## Step 3: Create compliant listings
Select the correct taxonomy path and complete all category-required attributes. Use an accurate title: Brand + model/product type + key differentiator. State inclusive selling price, MRP, stock, GST treatment, country of origin, warranty and package contents. Do not duplicate listings or claim features not supported by manufacturer evidence.

## Step 4: Upload images
Use at least three clear images, minimum 1000 px on the longest side, JPG/PNG, plain primary background and no watermark, contact details or misleading badges. The primary image must show the sold item. Fashion needs front/back or detail photos and a size chart; Electronics needs ports/model label where useful.

## Step 5: Price and fulfil orders
Selling price must not exceed displayed MRP and must include mandatory taxes. Do not add hidden charges. Maintain accurate inventory, pack securely, attach the correct invoice and hand over only through approved pickup. Dispatch by the promised handling date.

## Compliance checklist
- Identity, PAN/GST and bank verification complete
- Correct category, attributes, images and price published
- Product is genuine, safe and legally saleable in India
- Warranty, expiry and safety information disclosed where applicable
- No prohibited, counterfeit, recalled or restricted goods
- Returns, customer messages and statutory invoices handled on time

## Enforcement
Listings may be paused for inaccurate data, poor images, pricing violations, counterfeit concerns or repeated late dispatch. Serious or repeated violations may lead to account suspension and regulatory reporting.
