# Electronics Attribute Guide

Every Electronics listing must provide brand, exact model, MRP, selling price, GST-inclusive status, seller ID and warranty. Use SI units and manufacturer-confirmed values only.

## Wireless Earbuds
Required: Bluetooth version, total playback/battery life, charging port, driver size, ANC status (Yes/No and dB only when verified), microphone count, water resistance, compatibility and warranty. Do not call environmental-noise cancellation "ANC".

## Smartwatches
Required: display type/size, compatible operating systems, battery backup, charging time, health features, GPS status, water-resistance rating and warranty.

## Power accessories
Power banks require rated capacity, input/output wattage, ports and safety certifications. Chargers require wattage, connector and compatibility.
