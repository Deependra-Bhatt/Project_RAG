# Fashion Attribute Guide

Every Fashion listing must state brand, product type, material composition, colour, size availability, fit, care instructions, country of origin and seller ID.

## Apparel
Provide chest/waist/length measurements in centimetres and a standard India size chart. Describe fit as slim, regular, relaxed or oversized. Use product photos that show the actual colour; small colour variation across screens must be disclosed.

## Footwear and handbags
Footwear must include UK/India size, upper and sole material, closure and care. Handbags must include dimensions, material, compartments and strap type. Personalised apparel and made-to-order items must carry the `personalised-or-made-to-order` tag.
